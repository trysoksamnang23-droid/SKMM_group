Comprehensive End-to-End Human Resource Solutions

SKMM Investment Group delivers a seamless 360-degree service model connecting Cambodian candidates with accredited Japanese employers. We manage every step of the journey—from nation-wide talent sourcing and intensive Japanese linguistic training, to Certificate of Eligibility (COE) clearance, embassy visa facilitation, and continuous on-site welfare monitoring across Japan.

📋

1. Candidate Recruitment

ការជ្រើសរើសបេក្ខជន

Broad outreach campaigns across 25 Cambodian provinces through our university networks, vocational institutes, and community partnerships, attracting motivated, hardworking youth with clean legal records.

🔍

2. Candidate Assessment

ការវាយតម្លៃបេក្ខជន

Multi-stage screening comprising Uchida-Kraepelin aptitude tests, dexterity evaluations, comprehensive medical diagnostics at accredited hospitals, and in-depth background checks.

🤝

3. Job Matching

ការផ្គូផ្គងបេក្ខជនជាមួយការងារ

Precision matching between company job profiles and candidate strengths in agriculture, manufacturing, elderly care, hotel management, construction, and food processing.

🎌

4. Japanese Language Training

ការបណ្តុះបណ្តាលភាសាជប៉ុន

400 to 600 hours of intensive curriculum at HANABI Training Center. Trainees achieve JLPT N4 / JFT-Basic A2 proficiency, mastering workplace conversation (Kaiwa) and practical terminology.

💼

5. Interview Preparation

ការត្រៀមសម្ភាសន៍

Coaching in formal Japanese business etiquette (Aisatsu, Ojigi, Hou-Ren-So), professional grooming, and simulated mock interviews conducted with native Japanese advisors.

📄

6. Document Preparation

ការរៀបចំឯកសារ

Meticulous legal document compilation: certified Khmer-Japanese translations, curriculum vitae, civil registry verification, and official Ministry of Labour (MoLVT) clearance letters.

✈️

7. COE & Visa Support

ការគាំទ្រ COE និង Visa

Full cooperation with Japanese Regional Immigration Bureaus for Certificate of Eligibility (COE) issuance, followed by official visa processing through the Embassy of Japan in Phnom Penh.

🛫

8. Pre-departure Training

ការបណ្តុះបណ្តាលមុនចេញដំណើរ

Practical orientation on Japanese garbage separation, public transport navigation, earthquake & disaster preparedness, bank account management, and Japanese legal rights.

🛡️

9. Post-arrival Support

ការគាំទ្របន្ទាប់ពីទៅដល់ជប៉ុន

Dedicated Japan-based bilingual liaison officers conducting regular company visits, 24/7 hotline assistance, mental health counseling, and medical emergency translation.

The 6-Step Journey to Japan — ដំណាក់កាលដំណើរការទាំង ៦

From day one of enrollment to successful career completion in Japan:

1
Step 1: Registration & Medical Screening (ខែទី ១)
Application submission, physical medical examination, interview aptitude assessment, and enrollment at HANABI Center.
2
Step 2: Japanese Employer Interview (ខែទី ២)
Direct online video interview or in-person recruitment session with Japanese supervising organization and employer delegates.
3
Step 3: Intensive Japanese & Skills Mastery (ខែទី ៣ - ៥)
Achieving JLPT N4 / JFT-Basic A2 certification, trade skills training (agriculture, caregiving, food processing), and 5S workplace mindset.
4
Step 4: COE & Visa Application (ខែទី ៤ - ៦)
Japanese Immigration Bureau processes Certificate of Eligibility (COE); Embassy of Japan issues work visa; MoLVT issues flight departure permit.
5
Step 5: Send-off & Arrival in Japan (ខែទី ៦)
Pre-departure briefing, official family send-off ceremony at Phnom Penh International Airport, airport pickup in Japan, and dormitory move-in.
6
Step 6: Career & Reintegration (៣ ទៅ ៥ ឆ្នាំ)
Earn competitive Japanese income ($1,400 - $2,400/mo), gain advanced certifications, with lifetime career reintegration support back in Cambodia.
