Why Choose SKMM — ហេតុអ្វីគួរជ្រើសរើស SKMM

1. Legal and Transparent Company (法令遵守と高い透明性)

We operate in strict conformity with Cambodia's Labour Law and Japanese OTIT/Immigration regulations. We enforce a zero illegal broker fee policy, ensuring candidates arrive in Japan motivated, financially unburdened, and focused on their duties.

2. Professional Candidate Selection (厳格な人物・適性検査)

Our rigorous 5-stage selection includes Uchida-Kraepelin psychometric assessments, dexterity tests, criminal background clearances, and strict health checks at Japan-certified medical facilities.

3. Japanese Language Education (実践的な日本語教育)

At HANABI Japanese Language Center, candidates undergo 400 to 600 hours of intensive study before departure, attaining JLPT N4 / JFT-Basic A2 proficiency, with daily Kaiwa conversation and 5S workplace habits.

4. Continuous Support in Japan (手厚い日本現地サポート)

Our bilingual staff stationed in Tokyo and Osaka provide constant monitoring, assisting Japanese host companies with cultural translation, workplace adjustments, and 24/7 hotline support.

Candidate Selection Standards — ស្តង់ដារជ្រើសរើសបេក្ខជន

Every candidate proposed to Japanese employers must satisfy our rigorous vetting framework:

  • Document & Background Verification (身元・経歴調査): Complete verification of identity, family background, police criminal clearance certificate, and educational records.
  • Work Experience Check (職歴・実務能力確認): Practical evaluations matching Japanese employer specifications (e.g. welding tests, agricultural equipment, cooking assessments).
  • Japanese Language Assessment (語学力評価): Mandatory achievement of JLPT N4 or JFT-Basic A2, plus passing industry-specific Japanese vocabulary exams (e.g. Nursing Care Japanese).
  • Personality & Psychological Assessment (適性検査): Uchida-Kraepelin calculation test, teamwork evaluation, respect for rules, and emotional resilience under pressure.
  • Comprehensive Health Examination (精密健康診断): Full medical clearance including chest X-rays, blood analysis, hepatitis, tuberculosis, color blindness, and spinal ergonomics.
  • Motivation & Commitment (目的意識・継続意志): Personal interviews assessing career goals, family support, and commitment to completing full contract periods without absence.
