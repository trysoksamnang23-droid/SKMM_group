Company Profile — ប្រវត្តិក្រុមហ៊ុន

SKMM INVESTMENT GROUP CO., LTD. was founded in Phnom Penh, Cambodia, as an officially licensed Private Recruitment and Training Agency approved by the Ministry of Labour and Vocational Training (MoLVT) under License No. 048/19 MLVT.

Our core mission is to empower ambitious Cambodian youths with international career opportunities in Japan under the Technical Intern Training Program (TITP) and the Specified Skilled Worker (SSW / Tokutei Ginou) framework. Over the past decade, we have developed a comprehensive human resource ecosystem that combines ethical recruitment, rigorous Japanese language and vocational training at our proprietary HANABI Training Center, thorough pre-departure orientation, and 24/7 welfare monitoring in Japan.
