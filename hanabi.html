About HANABI Training Center — អំពីមជ្ឈមណ្ឌលបណ្តុះបណ្តាល ហាណាប៊ី

HANABI Training Center is the premier Japanese language academy and vocational preparation campus operated by SKMM Investment Group. Located in Phnom Penh, HANABI is dedicated to transforming Cambodian students and prospective technical trainees into confident, culturally fluent, and skilled professionals ready to succeed in Japan.

Our pedagogy integrates rigorous daily Japanese language training (listening, speaking, reading, writing) with practical immersion in Japanese corporate culture, the 5S methodology (Seiri, Seiton, Seiso, Seiketsu, Shitsuke), and essential workplace communication (Hou-Ren-So: Report, Contact, Consult).

Classrooms & Facilities — ថ្នាក់រៀន និងបរិក្ខារ

Designed to replicate Japanese academic and workplace standards:

  • 12 Modern Classrooms: Equipped with audiovisual projectors, digital whiteboards, and air conditioning.
  • Computer Prometric Mock Lab: 30 dedicated computer stations for practicing official JFT-Basic CBT examinations.
  • Caregiving Simulation Room: Equipped with electric nursing beds, wheelchairs, and transfer aids imported from Japan.
  • Agricultural Practice Workshop: Practical greenhouse and hydroponic irrigation training equipment.

Student Dormitory — អន្តេវាសិកដ្ឋានសិស្ស

To foster discipline and immersive teamwork, trainees reside full-time in our secure on-campus dormitory:

  • Separate, monitored male and female dormitory buildings with 24/7 security.
  • Nutritious dining hall serving 3 balanced meals daily prepared by licensed cooks.
  • Study halls, high-speed Wi-Fi, laundry facilities, and medical first-aid clinic.
  • Strict schedule adherence: morning exercise (Radio Taiso), room inspection (5S), and evening study hours.

Student Activities — សកម្មភាពសិស្ស

Holistic education beyond textbooks to develop cultural fluency and camaraderie:

  • Japanese Cultural Festivals: Tanabata, Obon dance practices, and traditional Mochi-pounding.
  • Speech Contests: Semi-annual Japanese eloquence competitions with cash prizes and trophies.
  • Cooking Workshops: Learning Japanese cuisine (Sushi, Curry rice, Miso soup, Bento preparation).
  • Physical Fitness Drills: Daily morning workouts and sports tournaments to build physical endurance.
